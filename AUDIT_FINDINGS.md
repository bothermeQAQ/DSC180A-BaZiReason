## P2 findings and improvements

- **Correlated uncertainty:** original `metrics.py::wilson` is mathematically descriptive, but its interval does not account for groups. Added paired whole-group bootstrap intervals with fixed seed and explicit small-sample limitations. This does not create population representativeness.
- **Prefix pilot:** original `cli.py` selects the first4 ordinary cases. It was already labeled only a network smoke test. Added exact ID selection and `audit_pilot_ids.json` covering all categories/statuses, plus `scripts/run_pilot.py` to avoid error-prone shell assembly.
- **Reference lineage and seconds:** lunar-python/sxtwl agree partly but share ShouXing ancestry. Independent JPL/Skyfield results support lunar-python more closely for24Jie 2024-25, without identifying the offset's cause or changing all labels into independently established truth.
- **H1 sample composition:** the revised sample remains purposive and ordinary/boundary strata are not fully matched. H1 needs year/time/task controls or restrained descriptive wording. This is documented, not falsely repaired by a larger count.
- **Finite scope:** no true solar time/local-civil chart profile, semantic explanation adjudication, native Anthropic adapter, model training or broad population inference has been added. These are scope choices, not invented correctness bugs.
