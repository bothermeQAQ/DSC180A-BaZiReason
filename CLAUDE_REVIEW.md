## Implementation assumptions and correctness risks

All pillars use fixed UTC+8 after legal civil instant normalization. International cases test normalization under this reference-clock convention, not birthplace-local BaZi casting. tzdata 2026.6 is forced via an empty zoneinfo search path. The engine accepts reference years 1901-2099; this is a study restriction, not an upstream accuracy guarantee.

Sect 1 day advances at 23:00, sect 2 at midnight; both retain the library's 23:00-advanced hour stem. This is deliberately implementation behavior, not a universal calendrical assertion. Missing profiles request clarification even when outputs could coincide. True solar time and several alternative rules are unsupported. Verify that the rejection policy and precision requirements are defensible.

Ground-truth labels are generated by lunar-python. sxtwl shares ShouXing ancestry, its supplied fork docs are old, and its PyPI source lineage/license metadata differ from the inspected fork. HKO supplies only tiny partial external checks. No complete independent four-pillar chart validation exists. Exact-second boundary tests only check the chosen engine's contract; they do not settle astronomical correctness.

## Leakage, fairness and measurement

Direct messages do not read expected values, boundary annotations or validation status. Unit tests mutate gold fields and verify prompt independence. The tool context intentionally includes answers; improvement would measure faithful incorporation of supplied facts, not autonomous reasoning. Both arms share rules and schema; longer context remains an inherent difference. No few-shot examples are used.

Case groups are correlated and must remain together during future splitting/bootstrap. The public suite may be contaminated by calendar facts in pretraining; synthetic dates do not guarantee novelty. There is no train split and no training yet. Mock adapters do not read gold labels. Metrics retain missing/failed observations and reject duplicate identities. Non-answer policy accuracy does not semantically verify reason wording; detectable contradiction rate is deliberately narrow.
