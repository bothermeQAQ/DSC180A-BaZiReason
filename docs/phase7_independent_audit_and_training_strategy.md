# BaZiReason after Phase 6

**Independent research audit and proposed training experiment · 21 November 2026**

## Executive summary

**Keep U8 as the reference model. Test whether a 4B model can learn to pass a computed value from one BaZi procedure to another.** Start with a three-arm continuation from the identical U8 adapter. Compare ordinary replay, training on a diverse set of compositions with conventional worked answers, and the same compositions with short, verifiable intermediate states. Reserve entire directed combinations of skills for assessment. Keep the primary endpoint a single model-alone answer to an ordinary question.

The headline counts hold. U8 answered 1,336/1,441 rule questions correctly. The four held-out combination families supplied **83 of its 105 errors**, despite accounting for only 11.1% of the items. There is direct evidence for a narrower diagnosis than “poor multi-step reasoning.” On fresh practice questions U8 got 100/100 on flow years and 100/100 on life stages. On 12 of its 20 *development* flow-year/life-stage errors, the reply explicitly looked up the natal month branch and gave the correct stage for that branch. The question required it to calculate and use the branch of the requested year. Other replies compute the first value correctly, then skip a membership test or count extra positions. These are observable failures of selection, scope and value transfer. Their relative contributions on new procedures remain unknown.

Two findings change the training decision. First, **the U8 lineage used the S2 recipe**. The final project narrative says it saw the same rule blocks and balanced reference-check training as S3. The manifests, training rows and checkpoint lineage contradict that account. Second, rule text produced **48 fixes and 24 regressions on combinations**, alongside **zero fixes and 189 regressions on template and blind questions combined**. The sign of the aggregate is largely determined by the mix of tasks. A change to evidence training deserves its own controlled trial.

U4c leaves room for a useful composition intervention. Its held-out combination gain was +1 point on development and +6.9 points on the historical test, with the latter 95% interval spanning −2.5 to +16.2. It tested six fixed procedures, one training seed and one 10% token substitution. The next trial should identify whether *which procedures are combined* or *how the intermediate result is supervised* drives transfer. Calendar decisions and self-review need different endpoints. The first pilot would take approximately **6–7 training hours** on the local M5, plus evaluation. All Phase 7 budgets, thresholds and allocations below are proposals.

## 3. Failure mechanisms and competing explanations

### A. Combining rules

The test samples four equally represented, fixed task families, two wording templates per family and an explicit JSON hint. New charts test new *instances* of these same four withheld operations. The generator and rules are public within the project. The development and validation families were repeatedly visible during research. The test protects item-level performance after selection, with limited evidence about transfer to a fifth, independently specified family. [Definitions, wordings and scorer](../src/bazireason/splits6.py) · [Split construction](../scripts/phase6/build_eval6.py)

| Historical family | Operation the answer requires | U8 alone /40 | With rule text /40 |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Flow-year stage | Calculate the requested year’s branch, pass it to the day master’s life-stage lookup | 5 | 26 |
| Void positions | Compute the void pair, test each eligible natal branch, return the matching **positions** | 20 | 32 |
| Revealed stems | Intersect a specified branch’s hidden stems with the three eligible visible stems | 24 | 20 |
| God count | Classify three visible stems relative to the day master, count only the requested class | 28 | 23 |

**1. Selecting the operand is the leading explanation for flow-stage errors.** In development reply `P6D-combo-00005`, U8 correctly enumerates the twelve stages for 丙, looks up natal month 辰, and returns 冠带. The question asks for 2043, whose branch is 亥 and stage is 绝. I counted a wrong-operand signature only when a wrong reply explicitly said “在上面找…支” and its parsed answer matched the rule for that named natal branch. U8 matched the month-branch signature 12 times in 20 misses. U4 matched the year branch 20 times in 21 misses, U4c 22/22, and U9 matched the hour branch 11 times in 20 misses. The studied years (2026–2045) already lie inside U2’s original training range. A blanket inability to do out-of-range year arithmetic fits these observations poorly. The written signature identifies an external behavior. It cannot show whether the model ever computed the requested year internally. [Development replies](../results/phase6/dev6/p6u8/combo_L0.jsonl) · [Training operand choices in `concrete_intent`](../src/bazireason/train_data6.py) · [Matched fit sample B](../results/phase6/fit_p6u8_b.json)

**2. Several later operations fail even after a usable intermediate appears.** On `P6D-combo-00014`, U8 derives the correct void pair 寅、卯, then lists all three positions, although none belongs to the pair. On `P6D-combo-00020`, it identifies the single visible wealth stem, extends the count to hidden stems, and returns two. On `P6D-combo-00007`, a revealed-stems question, it begins a pattern-style main-stem/Ten-God procedure. These examples implicate membership, aggregation and task choice at different points. Of the 83 sealed combo misses, 78 had a parsed JSON object. Formatting accounts for only a small part of the gap. The historical `first_error.py` compares text to a canonical rendering and skips unsupported derivations. A different first line may be semantically valid. It is unsuitable as a universal causal attribution for combinations. [Development items](../data/phase6/dev6/combo.jsonl) · [First-difference analyzer](../scripts/phase6/first_error.py)

**3. Output priors can mimic partial success.** On dev6 an always-empty list answers 13/25 void-position and 15/25 revealed-stem questions. Zero answers 12/25 god counts. These three fixed predictions alone get 40/75, before attempting any flow-stage question. The independent base got 56/160 sealed combinations despite only 12.9% on all rule items. These observations justify an answer-prior baseline and forced nonempty/positive cases. They do not establish how many U8 answers were guesses. The combo scorer accepts normalized strings for lists and coerces counts with `int`; for instance, a string containing no recognized position can normalize to an empty list. Archive the legacy metric; require typed integers and lists, plus independently adjudicated semantic equivalence, for future reward training.

**4. U4c was a limited transfer test.** It used 1,719 composition rows (600,133 estimated tokens), reducing procedure tokens from 3,000,061 to 2,400,262. Its six tasks were four natal stage lookups, clash→hidden stems→gods, luck direction→first pillar→god, top element→class, pair relations plus main gods, and stage plus seasonal state. The last two include parallel answers; the natal-stage task repeats a lookup at named positions. Several teach real dependencies, but the available operand sources, endpoint operations, two fixed wordings per kind and distinct output keys co-vary. A model can learn six more templates without learning to bind an arbitrary computed input. Six pattern questions on one held-out wording took the short, wrong answer shape in U4c. The implementation deliberately excluded the four test combinations and their terminal operation on the same inputs. [Composition builder](../src/bazireason/compose6.py) · [U4c manifest](../data/phase6/train/u4c/manifest.json) · [Development log](phase6_log.md)

U4c−U4 was 36−35/100 on dev6 with a paired interval of [−12, +14] points, and 60−49/160 on the historical split [−2.5, +16.2]. Aggregate rule accuracy differed by only +0.2 points on the latter. The two arms shared the U3v parent and seed. U4 and U4c nonetheless took 2,579 and 2,741 optimizer updates, saw 3.726M and 3.626M loss-bearing tokens, and selected iterations 5,000/5,158 and 5,483/5,483. On development, U4c gained five void-position answers net and lost four god counts. The observed small pooled change is consistent with redistribution, a weak transfer effect or an underpowered comparison. There is no dose curve, seed replication or factorial crossing of structure and target style. A definitive explanation of H11 requires those controls.

### B. Using supplied information

**The exposure history materially changes the diagnosis.** The November 20 log calls U2’s data recipe S3. The seven selected stages are U2→U3v→U4→U5→U6→U7→U8. Every manifest says `data_recipe: s2`. I scanned *user turns* of every training row on that path for the literal headers `[已核验的原始事实]`, `[规则说明]`, `[参考结论]` and `[规则代码已核验的结论]`. It yielded zero rows for each. The same scan on S3 yielded respectively 344, 203, 230 and 0. S3 trained on a “reference conclusion” header that differs from L3’s “code-verified” header. The generic `verify` family in U8’s manifest is legacy replay, distinct from S3’s `verify_reference`. The model has seen the rule content in worked answers and other verification tasks. Specific exposure to the Phase 6 evidence interfaces is the disputed point. [Build selection](../scripts/phase6/build_train6.py) · [U2 manifest](../data/phase6/train/u2/manifest.json) · [U8 manifest](../data/phase6/train/u8/manifest.json) · [S3 manifest](../data/phase6/train/s3/manifest.json) · [Conflicting account](phase6_log.md)

The levels are separate prompts. L1 prepends raw symbol attributes, L2 prepends chart-independent text selected by the *gold intent*, and L3 prepends the expected JSON. L2 changes rule availability, an intent cue, prompt length, position and vocabulary together. U8 gets +24 net on combinations and −202 net on the other 1,281 items (1,259→1,057). Only flow-stage and void positions improve within combinations. The per-part table also qualifies the claim that help only improves combinations. L3 raises independent items from 275 to 280 and counterfactual items from 155 to 158. Most lost template/blind answers at L2 still parsed (167/189). Across all rules, outputs reaching the generation cap rose from 5 at L0 to 32 at L2. Length and serialization contribute, but cannot account for the loss. [Prompt constructors and rule keys](../src/bazireason/ladder.py) · [Saved U8 ladder results](../results/phase6/sealed6/p6u8)

The paired L1/L2/L3 differences against L0 were respectively −1.9 [−3.3, −0.6], −12.4 [−14.6, −10.0] and −2.2 [−3.7, −0.8] percentage points. L3 labels the block as code-verified while retaining the model’s normal instruction to give a derivation. It lacks the Phase 5 instruction to adopt the conclusion directly. G1 and A1 gained 12.3 and 9.4 points with this L3 prompt, and the untuned base gained 12.0 points with L2. The context effect depends on the trained model, the task and the contract for using the supplied information. [Historical comparisons](phase6_round2_report.md) · [Phase 5 prompting conditions](phase5_report.md)

The most plausible combination of explanations is a useful reminder or task cue on unfamiliar joins, alongside a distribution shift that disturbs highly practiced routines on familiar items. Other live explanations include distracting tables, instruction order, omitted convention details, a poor choice among competing derivations, and inadequate exposure to the interface. The 98.3% baseline also leaves few opportunities to fix familiar tasks. S3’s dev L1 penalty shrank to −1.9 points versus S2’s −7.7, but S3 changed multiple mixture shares. G1 followed a supplied wrong conclusion on 814/1,329 eligible items versus U8 on 122/1,329. The full correct scores on those wrong-reference prompts were 124 and 1,050 respectively; most U8 misses there were not exact adoption of the supplied error. Learning to copy may buy agreement at the expense of independent checking. No attention measurement, clean length control or evidence-necessity comparison exists here.

### C. Self-review

| Check applied to U8’s L0 reply | Fixed among all 105 wrong | Broke among 300 sampled right | Estimated change if all 1,441 were reviewed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Neutral recheck | 11 | 16 | −4.65 points |
| Told there is an error | 16 | 26 | −8.00 points |
| Given a field to revisit | 11 | 22 | −6.67 points |

All initially wrong replies were reviewed, plus a seeded random sample of **60 correct replies per part**. The final column is my design-weighted estimate: within each part, subtract its total correct count times the observed break fraction from its observed fixes, sum and divide by 1,441. It has sampling uncertainty for unreviewed correct replies. For the neutral check, template and blind each broke 5/60 and fixed none. Combinations fixed 10/83 and broke 5/60 sampled from 77 right. The overall P-6 target was ≥25% fixes and ≤2% breaks; the model missed both. At this baseline, eleven fixes would be erased by breaking just 11/1,336 (0.82%) of initially correct answers. A “confident” assertion of error was even more costly. L4c uses gold fields to locate differences on wrong answers and can challenge a correct field on right answers. Treat it as a diagnostic. [Review sampling](../scripts/phase6/run6.py) · [Field selection and prompts](../src/bazireason/ladder.py)

Final flips conflate detection, localization, revision and susceptibility to the instruction. There are no calibrated error probabilities in these records. S3’s neutral recheck broke 0/278 sampled right on development, but fixed only 2/184 wrong. A policy that always keeps its answer would also look safe on the first statistic. Its self-check supervision labels a differing *text line* on incorrect replies and can affirm all steps when the final answer scores right. Semantically equivalent wording or a lucky correct answer can teach the wrong lesson. A useful future test asks for a keep/revise decision before a new answer, scores localization against a semantic checker, and compares the resulting final accuracy to independent second generation at the same decode budget. Record wrong→different-wrong as well as right/wrong transitions. [S3 review target construction](../src/bazireason/train_data6.py)

### D. Calendar decisions and arithmetic

U8 refused 28/531 answerable requests and gave definite charts on 15/285 requests requiring another status. The other 22 errors had the correct decision to answer but wrong pillars. Within those 22, 20 missed day and hour only, one missed day only, and one missed all four. Twelve were plain chart requests. This accounts for all 65 misses. The score identifies a definite response; it does not measure a numerical confidence. Balanced decision accuracy was 94.7%. [Calendar records](../results/phase6/sealed6/p6u8/calendar_L0.jsonl) · [Calendar score definitions](../src/bazireason/p2_eval.py)

The day stem feeds the hour stem, so counting each wrong field as an independent failure exaggerates the evidence. Nor can all 22 earliest errors be assigned to day arithmetic. In archived `P6S-calendar-00003` the request gives December **2019** in New York. The answer converts the clock to December **2020** before doing any day-pillar calculation. This is a concrete upstream input error. For the other cases, trace the first divergence through copied date, reference time/rollover, leap-day count, day-of-year, mod 60 and hour derivation. The score flags alone cannot do that. [Worked day formula and conversion](../src/bazireason/worked.py)

The abstention problem has separate evidence. In the development logs U3v/U4/U5 made 9/5/11 refusals contradicting their own checks, out of 18/19/26 calendar errors. The Shanghai “needs tool” fit cell oscillated 10→26→7→29→11 correct of 32 from U2 through U6 along the selected path as the sampling weights changed. This suggests prevalence sensitivity. It does not prove loss of the underlying timezone rule. A controlled twin design can change only the fact that determines answerability, holding the calendar margin stable. Correct date transfer and checking before committing may help both domains. Modulo arithmetic and calibrated abstention each deserve a separate intervention and endpoint. ## 4. Which methods merit a trial?

The comparisons below concern learned behavior of the same Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 base. A rule engine can generate labels, construct contrasts and score outputs offline. The headline measure gives the deployed model a question and chart, with a fixed output contract. In particular, reward-model selection among several inference-time answers would be a separate system result.

| Priority and method | Mechanism and research evidence | Local cost, regression risk and discriminating test |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Vary the computation graph and supervise intermediate values** | Recombine compatible operations while changing the source of their inputs. Requiring explicit operands, membership decisions and reductions gives a learning signal exactly where dev answers go astray. Andreas found that fragment substitution improved SCAN and semantic parsing; Yao and Koller showed that the distribution of synthetic meaning representations substantially affected transfer. Nye et al. found that emitting intermediate state helps on algorithmic tasks. These experiments support changing the representation and sampling distribution. They supply no effect-size forecast for BaZi. [Andreas, ACL 2020](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.676/) · [Yao & Koller, NAACL 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.naacl-long.25/) · [Nye et al., research preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.00114) | Works with existing masked-completion LoRA, labels from code and one GPU. Generator and independent checking are the main setup costs. New templates could simply replace old ones, and verbose states could encourage copying. Compare the same novel training graphs with conventional prose versus explicit values. Test withheld edges, altered surface forms and source-switch pairs under L0. |
| **2. Evidence-necessity and distractor training** | Present clean, irrelevant and conflicting context with an explicit convention for which source is authoritative. Include pairs where the correct answer genuinely changes only because supplied evidence changes. Li et al. improved controllability and robustness with counterfactual/irrelevant QA contexts on T5 and PaLM. Controlled instruction-tuning experiments show that consistent context alone can reinforce reliance on parameters. Local L2 usually repeats facts the model could derive, so a faithful answer gives little evidence that the text was read. [Li et al., Findings of ACL 2023](https://aclanthology.org/2023.findings-acl.112/) · [Context-Parametric Inversion, ICLR 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/aa27ac7aca4e462da1439b43ceebc04c-Abstract-Conference.html) | One subsequent SFT fork and a matched context-exposure control. Longer prompts reduce completed examples per token. Overtraining on authoritative text could reproduce G1’s wrong-reference following. Test evidence-required pairs, trusted facts, untrusted suggestions and equal-length distractors on new operations; gate on L0 retention. |
| **3. Fixed replay and decision-before/after ablation for calendar** | Interleave familiar tasks throughout training; compare calendar twins with the deciding check written before versus after the verdict. Replay has empirical continual-learning support, largely outside LLM domain adaptation. The local H9 fork and UD generator checks make ordering testable. Experiments on hallucination snowballing found that an early wrong commitment can induce later rationalization. These are reasons to test ordering; they do not establish how U8 computes. [Rolnick et al., NeurIPS 2019](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2019/hash/fa7cdfad1a5aaf8370ebeda47a1ff1c3-Abstract.html) · [Zhang et al., ICML 2024](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/zhang24ay.html) | Use replay as a common retention control in the first experiment. Test order separately with the same clauses, dates, decisions and family weights. Equalize verdict frequencies within feasible cells. Measure false refusals, definite answers on nonanswerable twins, and exact arithmetic among answerable rows. Replay may preserve the shortcut too; UD changes relations and domain targets as well, so running the entire UD package would leave calendar attribution ambiguous. |
| **4. Offline contrastive preferences and selective review** | Collect plausible U8 successes and failures. Prefer a trace with the right operand over a length-matched trace with the wrong operand; include “keep” pairs. DPO optimizes a preference against a reference policy without online rollouts during fitting. Its original evidence is mainly preference alignment. Lightman et al. showed a benefit for step-labeled mathematical *verifiers*; that result alone gives no basis for expecting better single-sample BaZi generation. [Rafailov et al., NeurIPS 2023](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/a85b405ed65c6477a4fe8302b5e06ce7-Abstract.html) · [Lightman et al., ICLR 2024](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/aca97732e30bcf1303bc22ac3924fd16-Abstract-Conference.html) | Requires pair construction and a new MLX loss or trainer; cache reference log-probabilities and score the frozen reference serially. Pairs consume at least two policy sequences per prompt. Style, length and edit frequency are easy preference shortcuts. Compare DPO to chosen-only SFT on the same pool and equal total budget, including generation. Test single-pass new graphs plus review at matched edit coverage and decode cost. |
| **5. Verified sampling, then conditional on-policy RL** | Use rejection sampling to estimate whether U8 already generates correct paths. Verified successful trajectories could be distilled; online groups can instead reward correct intermediate transitions and final answers. STaR demonstrated benefits from training on successful rationales. RLVR studies disagree about capability expansion: one NeurIPS study found gains concentrated at low sample counts in its settings; ProRL reported broader gains with prolonged RL on a small reasoning model. Deterministic labels make BaZi promising, while its skewed answers make terminal rewards easy to exploit. [Zelikman et al., NeurIPS 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.14465) · [RLVR capability study, NeurIPS 2025](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/537d5aa768c2d534016a4d06f87bc8fb-Abstract-Conference.html) · [ProRL, NeurIPS 2025](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/1a22b912945fb7c0bdd079e792b31b6f-Abstract-Conference.html) | Four samples per prompt already multiply decoding by roughly four before updates. No ready project GRPO trainer was identified. Weak groups yield little reward contrast; accepting lucky empty answers or a parser loophole teaches the wrong behavior. Precondition on meaningful, semantically verified success and failure on training-only graphs. Compare rejection SFT first, then cost-matched RL if warranted. Report greedy pass@1, fixed-temperature pass@k and trace validity on unseen structures. |
| **6. Episodic compositional curricula** | Randomize operand sources or small rulebooks across episodes so a fixed surface cue stops predicting the answer. Lake and Baroni demonstrated systematic transfer using a dynamic stream of few-shot instruction tasks. In synthetic factual composition, Wang et al. found marked limits to out-of-distribution composition even after extended training. Episodes are a substantive change in task distribution and may require demonstrations at inference. [Lake & Baroni, Nature 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06668-3) · [Wang et al., NeurIPS 2024](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/ad217e0c7fecc71bdf48660ad6714b07-Abstract-Conference.html) | Feasible with offline synthesis and the same LoRA. Multi-example prompts cost context space under the 2,048-token training cap. Synthetic rulebooks could erode real conventions. Compare a shuffled curriculum to episodic ordering of the same examples and tokens. Require transfer back to real BaZi at L0 with held-out graph structures and no support examples. |

**Self-correction has a higher bar than producing a persuasive second answer.** Huang et al. found that feedback-free rechecking often degrades reasoning. SCoRe achieved improvements with on-policy multi-turn RL and regularization designed to prevent degenerate correction policies. Its larger-model math/code experiments establish feasibility in principle. U8’s low error prevalence, cheap oracle training labels and expensive local decoding favor a smaller experiment first: error detection plus keep/revise supervision on its own training-only drafts, evaluated at natural prevalence. If it cannot identify errors with enough precision to meet the measured break-even rate, expanding to two-turn RL is a poor use of this machine. [Huang et al., ICLR 2024](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/8b4add8b0aa8749d80a34ca5d941c355-Abstract-Conference.html) · [SCoRe, ICLR 2025](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/871ac99fdc5282d0301934d23945ebaa-Abstract-Conference.html)

## 5. Proposed Phase 7 experiment

### Hypotheses in priority order

| Hypothesis | Measurement that would strengthen it | Outcome that would change the plan |
|---|---|---|
| H1. The model often applies a familiar consumer to the wrong source. Varied graph coverage plus explicit operand/value targets improves that transfer. | On held-out directed edges, more correct operands and finals, especially when a natal and derived operand give different answers. Primary comparison is specified below. | If matched standalone primitives fail on the same charts, train that missing primitive first. If the right operand is consistently produced and the reducer fails, target membership/counting instead. |
| H2. Some context regressions reflect unfamiliar presentation; some gains reflect an intent cue or useful missing information. | A neutral header or token-matched irrelevant text reproduces part of the loss; a relevant rule exceeds a length-matched sham, and evidence-required twins follow the authorized fact in both directions. | If an intent name alone reproduces the whole benefit, prioritize task selection. If clean compact rules still hurt versus sham, inspect rule correctness, precedence and the first semantic divergence before scaling exposure. |
| H3. Family reweighting alters calendar decision priors, with separable arithmetic errors. | On a calendar-only fork, balanced contrasts and check-first targets improve both members of answerability twins; conditional day arithmetic can be measured independently. | A fall in false refusals matched by more unwarranted charts indicates a threshold shift. Correct status and copied reference dates with wrong remainders call for an arithmetic-specific fork. |
| H4. Self-review lacks a calibrated detection/keep decision and sometimes lacks the ability to repair after detection. | A blind error flag predicts original errors; accurate localization improves revision conditional on a true error; expected net accuracy rises on natural-prevalence drafts. | Good detection with failed repairs motivates localized revision training. Good forced repairs with low detector precision motivates calibration. Neither pattern warrants unconditional review. |

### Build an assessment that can survive the experiment

1. **Retire the Phase 6 seal.** Freeze its existing results as historical evidence. Exclude its prompts, replies, four canonical combo signatures and close paraphrases from Phase 7 training, curriculum design targets, checkpoint ranking and the new confirmatory endpoint. Fresh draws from those same four families would still test known structures. Also inventory prior replay, training/reference traces, fit samples, blind-writer pools and auxiliary labels for implicit solved subproblems. U8 has already absorbed its ancestry.
2. **Specify computation before writing questions.** Write a small typed grammar of valid operations on a chart, such as deriving a branch, reading hidden stems, classifying a symbol, testing membership, filtering positions and counting. Record a canonical directed graph, input roles, scope, reducer and answer type. Strip aliases and sort commutative siblings. Have a second implementation/person review semantic equivalences, including edges nested in a larger old target. Reserve separate sets of *edges and complete graphs* for training, development, validation and final. All component skills must appear in training or the frozen U8 skill inventory. Keep a distinct depth-generalization stratum. A natural new example is finding the element of the main hidden stem of a **derived** branch, with the branch source varied across equivalent questions. Its novelty must pass the inventory before assignment. Keysers et al.’s compound-divergence design motivates measuring primitive-frequency overlap and compound disjointness. [Keysers et al., ICLR 2020](https://research.google/pubs/measuring-compositional-generalization-a-comprehensive-method-on-realistic-data/)
3. **Separate structural and wording tests.** Cross familiar/new graphs with familiar/new phrasing and record both. Use disjoint chart keys and year blocks, date/solar-term-event and calendar-twin IDs, counterfactual parent IDs, writer pools and paraphrase templates. Put siblings and transformed equivalents in the same split. Deduplicate at these levels, including against earlier phases. In the audit, literal first-user matches against the selected seven training files were zero across the six historical test parts, but there was one repeated first-user string within the historical calendar set. Exact matching provides a useful floor for the stronger semantic audit. Reserve an independently written or human-reviewed question panel. The old “blind” questions came from another Qwen generator and no user-written panel was used.
4. **Control shortcuts.** Include reachable nonempty lists, ties, duplicate symbols with different positions, each feasible count, positive/negative predicates, and both projection and aggregation. Use matched inputs that differ only in which source to consult, including cases where sources disagree and cases where a distractor changes while the answer stays fixed. Match depth, output length, answer type and atom coverage across comparable splits. Report a balanced diagnostic and a separately defined natural-prevalence panel. Publish the constant-per-family and natal-default baselines fitted on training/dev, with their frozen predictions. If a family has no support outside a single label or cannot be split without prior exposure, replace it **before** locking.
5. **Independent labels and seal.** Cross-check gold transitions with a second implementation, lunar-python where applicable, and blinded review of a stratified sample of questions, especially school-dependent rules, changed operands and refusals. Strict typed scoring must reject booleans-as-counts, malformed strings that normalize to empty, duplicate positions, extraneous final keys where material, and conflicting conclusions. Freeze legitimate equivalent representations, caps and adjudication before seeing outputs. Hash split and scorer manifests, record ownership/access and keep final labels outside the training and selection process. Predeclare which alternative BaZi conventions are accepted. No draw from an opened split can become a fresh seal by renaming it.

### The first trial

**Before training, use a small Phase 7 development diagnostic.** On approximately 120 fresh graph–chart groups, ask the full question, both isolated primitives on that same chart, and a matched question with the correct intermediate supplied. Counterbalance an irrelevant intermediate of the same shape and an intent-only cue. Score exact answers and separately label operand/source, operation, intermediate value, reduction and serialization. Include source-switch twins with disagreeing gold answers. The rescue effect estimates sensitivity to supplying that information; it may also cue the procedure. Do not count rescued answers toward intrinsic performance. If most full-task errors occur on items where a prerequisite also fails, investigate that prerequisite before commissioning composition training.

| Arm | 80% common stream | Remaining 20% of the sequence-token budget | Comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R** | Identical fixed replay stream across arms | Familiar procedures in the established format | Active equal-compute continuation control; frozen U8 also evaluated |
| **G** | Same stream | Balanced training-only computation graphs, conventional numbered derivations and final JSON | G−R estimates effect of substituting the new training distribution |
| **T** | Same stream | Same graph questions, gold answers and semantic facts as G. Expose the operand source, value consumed by the next step, and each inclusion/reduction decision in compact fields | T−G estimates the effect of explicit state supervision with graph exposure held constant |

For example, two branches can share the same downstream lookup. A training target can record “source = branch opposite the specified natal branch”, “derived branch = …”, “main hidden stem of **that branch** = …”, then the requested final value. Its paired question changes the source while preserving consumer, answer schema and most words. Use legal domain operations and the project’s Chinese vocabulary. The model must generate the states at inference if it uses them. The L0 user sees an ordinary unannotated chart and question. Keep final-answer formatting identical for all arms. Score trace fields as secondary observations, allowing valid alternative derivations.

Start all arms from the checksum-verified selected U8. Use the same quantized base, rank, layers, batch, optimizer, learning-rate schedule and greedy evaluation settings. A concrete pilot is **3M sequence tokens per arm**, half a historical continuation. Allocate the common stream as 30% calendar, 38% familiar procedures, 8% existing contrasts, 4% other replay (shares of the total). Stratify calendar by family and status. Fix this mixture before seeing any results. Select a fixed update endpoint, with earlier snapshots used only for development diagnostics. Pair graph IDs, train/validation seeds and shuffles across G/T. Bin target lengths and pack/batch pairs to match optimizer updates, supervised tokens and padded tokens within a preregistered tolerance (proposed 3%). Match the count of distinct graph–chart examples exactly across G/T. Use equally informative conventional text and no meaningless padding as a loss target. If matching fails, redesign renderings before launch or declare the contrast a representation-plus-compute comparison. Log actual wall time, updates, input/loss-bearing tokens and truncated rows.

One matched seed for R/G/T is a feasibility screen on **development only**. If promising, run two further matched seeds for all three arms under the frozen recipe. Use validation once to apply the preset go/no-go rule. Use the fixed final update for each seed; never promote the luckiest seed. Evaluate the prespecified U8, R, G and T comparisons on the fresh seal in one campaign. T is the declared candidate. A surprising win by G is a result that can motivate a later confirmatory experiment, with no retrospective switch of the primary hypothesis. Fixed replay avoids feedback from parent errors changing the treatment between arms.

### Endpoints, uncertainty and decisions

- **Primary estimand.** Macro-average family L0 semantic final-answer accuracy on fresh structurally held-out graphs, equally weighting prespecified families then seeds. The practical decision is T−R. Require an estimated advantage of **at least 8 percentage points**, a paired 95% lower bound above zero, and a positive paired difference for each of the three seed pairs. Require T to improve over frozen U8 as well (lower bound above zero). A continuation that merely recovers R’s regression cannot qualify. Attribute extra benefit to structured targets only if the ordered secondary contrast T−G is positive with a 95% lower bound above zero. If G and T both win over R but their difference is unresolved, the supported result concerns graph coverage or the common composition package.
- **Retention gates.** Against frozen U8, require the paired lower 95% bound above **−1.5 points** on ordinary rule questions and **−2 points** on full calendar correctness. Also report answerable refusal, definite-on-nonanswerable, exact pillars conditional on correct status, counterfactual pairs both right, checker contradictions and format failures. Set per-family harm alerts prospectively (proposed >5-point loss on a sufficiently supported family triggers review and blocks adoption pending replication). Measure L1/L2/L3 and wrong-reference adoption on a separate fixed diagnostic panel even when the model is trained only at L0.
- **Size and precision.** Provisionally reserve ≥12 final graph families × 80 independent chart groups (960 groups), distinct dev/validation families, and around 1,200 each of familiar rule and calendar groups for final retention. If 25% of matched independent groups are discordant, a paired difference has approximate standard error ≤√(0.25/960), or 1.6 points; a 95% half-width is about 3.2 points *conditional on those families*. At 5% retention discordance and 1,200 independent groups the analogous half-width is about 1.3 points. Shared templates/events, heterogeneity across families and the small number of seeds widen uncertainty. Use a blind design simulation with dev-only discordance and family variance to check detection of an 8-point gain and both non-inferiority margins. Increase groups/families or declare the design underpowered **before** sealing. Extra paraphrases of the same event do not add independent groups.
- **Inference.** Join all models by immutable case ID. Average siblings within chart/twin group, then by family. Resample paired groups within families for conditional-on-suite intervals. Also resample families for the broader family-transfer interval; use the wider interval for the primary decision. Preserve repeated-seed predictions on each sampled group. Report per-family/per-seed deltas and leave-one-family-out sensitivity. With three seeds describe seed variation explicitly and avoid a precise population-of-seeds claim. Report pooled deployment-weighted accuracy separately. The primary and retention requirements are joint gates. Use ordered secondary tests, or Holm-adjust any familywise claims across several methods. Label exploratory diagnostics.
- **Stop rules.** Before training, stop for structural leakage, conflicting gold labels, unusable independent primitives, unsupported evaluation cells or a token/update mismatch outside tolerance. After the first seed, stop for futility if T−R ≤0 on fresh dev combinations **and** source-switch/transition accuracy shows no improvement; stop for harm if either retention point loss exceeds twice its margin. Do not spend the two replication seeds on an inconclusive pilot unless a prespecified promising signal is present (proposed T−R ≥4 points and both retention point estimates inside their margins). If gates remain inconclusive after confirmation, retain U8 and report the interval. A gain confined to seen graphs, cued prompts, permissive scoring, one seed or one family is insufficient for a generalization claim. Open the new seal only after the protocol, code, checksums and seed endpoints are frozen; retire it after this campaign.

### Follow-up experiments conditional on the result

**Evidence.** On newly generated dev items randomize five equal-interface conditions within item: no evidence, neutral matched filler, intent cue, minimal relevant rule, and a true irrelevant rule matched in length and vocabulary. Counterbalance before/after-question placement. Compare full tables to minimal rules and measure cap hits. Separately cross a clean versus corrupt field with “authoritative case fact” versus “fallible reference to verify.” Where the chart already fixes the answer, a false reference should be rejected. For a legitimate convention choice or missing case fact, vary the authorized value in paired prompts so the correct answer must change. Audit rule completeness against the oracle. Train a matched fork on context-always-consistent exposure versus mixed relevance and truth, holding L0 replay and supervised tokens fixed. Require clean-context gains over the control, preserved L0, accuracy on both members of necessity pairs, and low exact adoption of false suggestions. This can distinguish exposure, relevance and placement effects for the tested prompts. It cannot reconstruct attention or prove that the model internally trusts a source.

**Calendar.** Use only training-year pairs with one status-determining difference and identical surrounding context; independently validate with calendar logic and applicable exact timestamps. Vary the supplied offset, missing information, fold, solar-term window, and day-boundary convention. For arithmetic, pair neighboring dates on either side of leap-day/month/day rollover and score normalized timestamp, leap total, ordinal, remainder and final pillars. Compare order-only supervision against identical values in the original order; isolate numerical-state supervision in a later fork. Keep group marginals fixed. A better refusal prior without better twin-pair accuracy fails the boundary-learning hypothesis.

**Review.** Draw first replies from the current candidate on fresh training-only charts. Label semantic step errors and correct-but-unfaithful traces, include naturally correct replies and equal-length plausible wrong ones. On validation stratify by part and correctness, recording inclusion probabilities. Freeze the detector threshold on development. Report detection precision/recall, calibration (Brier score and reliability bins if probabilities are available), edit coverage, net fixes minus breaks at natural prevalence, uncertainty by chart, and repair success conditional on correct detection. Compare with always keep, independent re-answer, neutral recheck and oracle-location-only diagnostics. Require a positive lower bound on net accuracy after review at matched token budget and a non-inferior first pass before adopting a learned reviewer.

## 6. Compute, implementation risk and limits

The observed machine is an **Apple M5 MacBook Pro, 10 CPU cores, 10 GPU cores, 32 GB unified memory**, with about 353 GiB free at inspection. The checked-in U8 recipe uses the local 4-bit, group-size-64 Qwen model, rank-16 LoRA on the last 16 layers (14,680,064 trainable parameters), AdamW, batch two, two-way gradient accumulation, max length 2,048 and prompt masking. U8 processed 5,992,361 sequence tokens and 3,713,101 loss-bearing tokens in 14,627.5 seconds (4.06 h), using 19.97 GB peak. U2 took 10.56 h for 14.97M tokens. Checkpoints and adapters already occupy roughly 16 GB. Past runs exhausted unified memory when another local model was loaded. ## 7. Open questions and limits

**How far does the operand error extend?** The twelve month-branch matches directly establish one development failure mode. Measure correctly chosen source, correctly computed value and correctly applied downstream operation separately on matched new questions. Whether explicit intermediate-state training is the cheapest effective correction is an empirical question for T versus G.

**How much does training randomness matter?** Replicating a fork from frozen U8 estimates continuation-seed sensitivity. It leaves variation from pretraining and the entire earlier U2–U8 path unmeasured. Similarly, none of the archived checkpoints supplies an equal-budget control for every stage of the adaptive error-weighting rule.

**Would users see the same failure mix?** Historical scores measure the project’s operational conventions and structured answers. Independent lunar-python labels cover a subset. Human agreement on diverse, naturally phrased BaZi tasks and the real prevalence of combination requests remain unmeasured. A human-written panel and a stated deployment mixture would change the interpretation of aggregate facts, review break-even rates and hybrid comparisons.

**What can be concluded causally?** A randomized length-matched prompt intervention can identify the behavioral effect of its supplied cue. Paired training forks can identify the effect of the specified recipe under the tested conditions. Neither identifies an internal reasoning circuit. The finite graph inventory bounds claims about arbitrary composition, and a confident-sounding refusal provides no estimate of probabilistic calibration. Effects on expert interpretation or predictive validity lie outside these labels.

## Final recommendation

**First commission the fresh graph split and matched prerequisite diagnostic. Then, if the primitive checks pass, run the three-arm R/G/T pilot from U8.** The proposed 9M-token pilot buys two answers the 6M-token U4c fork could not give: whether wider coverage of valid dependencies transfers to explicitly withheld ones, and whether showing the source and consumed value adds anything beyond that coverage. Advance only on replicated, unaided gains with retained rule and calendar accuracy. Evidence integration is the next independent training question, especially given the corrected S2 lineage. Keep calendar repair, self-review and online reinforcement learning conditional on the specific failure each is intended to fix.
