# Phase 9 · Generalization of complete BaZi questions

November 22, 2026. Initial elapsed-time estimate 5–8 hours. The authorized campaign comprises three fixed continuations, frozen model comparisons and a report. No hyperparameter sweep, post-result retry with modified data, or automatic follow-on training.

## Selection of the comparison

Phase 8 improved the targeted single-step lookup substantially while leaving unaided complete questions weak. Its training predecessor G learned Ten-God graph answers under only two star producers. Some relations in its training data never requested either of the two hard downstream operations. The proposed explanation is that source choice helps predict what question is being asked. The model may rely on this correlation when wording shifts. A separate explanation is difficulty keeping the chosen operation and operands available through generation. Both are behavioral hypotheses. A third factor, executing the downstream lookup itself, is controlled by giving all new arms fully calculated worked answers.

Use G as parent in all arms. S uses the sparse producer-to-consumer support, X expands and rebalances that support, and P uses X’s questions and gold answers with explicit operation and operand supervision. Keep calendar, rule and graph replay, optimizer, shared seed, endpoint and training budget matched. Compare realized exposure, sequence, loss and padded tokens. Allow up to 3% budget discrepancy and require equal optimizer updates. Do not introduce the four new producer rules, three-step chains, or any of the four designated familiar-source held-out edges. New example wording must be authored independently from the evaluation. No evaluation outputs inform the training. Exact combinations previously exposed in Phase 7–8 *evaluation* may be used in X/P training except the designated holdouts. Prior evaluation scores for those combinations are henceforth development results.

## Panels and contrasts

The primary outcome is exact family-balanced accuracy on 192 independently worded two-step questions, comprising four new branch producers crossed with four consumers on 96 unseen charts. A fixed reference gives the unfamiliar producer tables to all models so they can execute the presented rule. Two different requested outputs share the chart and producer. The main causal contrast is X minus S. Report X minus G to distinguish improvement from deterioration of the control. P minus X is the secondary causal contrast for explicit plan/operand targets. These one-seed contrasts distinguish training packages subject to their realized exposure differences.

Further transfer checks use 96 unaided questions on new producers (paired to identical primary questions), other wording for the primary, changed requested operation on the same chart, matched isolated producer and consumer probes, operation identification, and unaided three-step questions. Focused bridge questions test unseen charts and independently written, unaided questions on four previously evaluated familiar-source edges. All three arms must exclude these four edges from training. Report separately by producer and operation, especially life stage and Ten God. Closed-book comparisons on new producers also require prior knowledge of those sources.

Evaluate S/X/P, G and Phase 8 A on all transfer and retention panels. U8 is an additional fresh rule/calendar reference. Use greedy generation, the project system prompt and historical caps and scorers. Never replace, rescue, or select replies by score. Record every prompt and response and verify all IDs, scores, hashes, and chart separations.

Predeclare +4 percentage points X−S with a paired chart 95% interval above zero and a positive X−G change as a promising primary result. For model selection report all three continuations and all contrasts. Prefer a candidate for future confirmation only when its point score exceeds G on primary and unaided bridge, and fresh familiar-rule and calendar point losses versus both G and U8 stay within 1.5 and 2 percentage points. Show uncertainty, refusal behavior and the deeper-chain result separately. Intervals quantify sampling of these synthetic charts. Resampling four producers is a sensitivity analysis, not a broad population or training-seed guarantee. No frontier benchmark is part of this experiment.
