# Evidence interpretation

## Phase 6 (`phase6/`): what the model can do alone

`phase6/RESULTS.md` and `phase6/summary.json` are generated by `scripts/phase6/make_report6.py` from the files below; it runs no model. The design is in `docs/phase6_preregistration.md` (later changes in its section 9), the working log in `docs/phase6_log.md` and the narrative in `docs/phase6_report.md`. **Every table names its level; L0 is the model alone, and L5 (the Phase 5 system) is never called model accuracy.**

- `phase6/<split>/<label>/<part>_<level>.jsonl`: one record per item, with the intent, the raw reply and the score (parsed answer, correct or not, held-out wording or not). The statement tables are computed from the raw replies by the report script.
  - **Splits:** `dev6` (read freely), `valid6` (selection only), `sealed6` (scored once, after `selection.json`).
  - **Parts:** `rules`, `indep`, `blind`, `combo` (held-out combinations of trained rules), `cf` (counterfactual pairs), `calendar`.
  - **Levels:** `L0` alone; `L1` raw facts; `L2` rule text; `L3` / `L3w` right / wrong conclusion; `L4a`, `L4b`, `L4c` self-check; `L5` the Phase 5 system (`scripts/phase6/run_l5.py`).
  - **Labels:** `base`, `p2`, `p4a1`, `p5g1`, the Phase 6 runs `p6u0`, `p6s1`, `p6s2`, `p6s3`, `p6u1`, `p6u1hi`, and the round-2 runs `p6u2` (short system prompt) and `p6u3`-`p6u5` (weighted continuations).
- **Decisions:** `screening.json` (dev6, the U1 recipe), `round2.json` (dev6: the adapter structure and each step of the continuation loop) and `selection.json` (valid6, the selected model), from `scripts/phase6/decide6.py`. `sealed_access.jsonl` logs every sealed run.
- **Diagnostics:**
  - `audit_repro.json`: the capability audit's numbers recomputed from saved records;
  - `diag_fit.json`, `fit_<label>.json`: accuracy on training rows and on training-distribution questions (under-fitting check). `fit_<label>_clean.json` counts only the questions whose chart the model never trained on, and `fit_<label>_b.json` is fit sample B (100 questions per question type, never training input);
  - `own_replies_<label>.jsonl`, `own_replies_<label>_b.jsonl`: a model's own replies to fit samples A and B (S1's replies to sample A are the input of the self-check rows);
  - `token_share.json`: how the training tokens split between system prompts, user turns and replies, and the tokenization check of the stems and branches;
  - `truncation.json`: replies cut off at the token cap;
  - `panel/<label>.jsonl`: a fixed panel scored at saved checkpoints;
  - `memory_probe.jsonl`: the U1-hi memory probe.
- `phase6/training/<run>/`: plan, loss reports and summary of each run. Weights are not in Git; the training sets are rebuilt bit for bit by `scripts/phase6/build_train6.py --check`.
- `phase6/cache/` (not in Git) caches every reply by prompt hash, so reruns reuse it.

## Phase 5 (`phase5/`): an engine-first grounded system

`phase5/RESULTS.md` and `phase5/summary.json` are generated by `scripts/phase5/make_report5.py` from the files below; it runs no model. The design is in `docs/phase5_preregistration.md`, and the narrative in `docs/phase5_report.md`.

- `phase5/<split>/<label>/<part>_<condition>.jsonl`: one record per item, with the prompt hash, the intent used and the true intent, the raw reply, the code's check and the score. `grv` records add the repair reply, its check, whether the engine's fallback was shown, and the final score. `.summary.json` files hold counts and timing.
  - **Splits:** `dev` (read freely; files tagged `_p2` use prompt version 2), `valid` (selection only), `sealed` (scored once).
  - **Labels:** `base`, `p2`, `p3r3`, `p4a1`, `p5g1`, and `mix-p3r3-p4a1-<α>` for interpolated adapters.
  - **Conditions:** `w0` model alone, `gf` full fact sheet, `go` oracle-routed, `gr` routed, `grv` routed and verified. `convo_<condition>.jsonl` holds five-turn dialogues.
- **Selection:** `dev_alpha.json` (the interpolation point) and `selection.json` (the merged adapter's gates and the generator), from `scripts/phase5/select.py`.
- `phase5/sealed_access.jsonl` logs every sealed run.
- `phase5/training/bazireason-qwen3-4b-lora-p5-g1/`: plan, loss reports and summary of G1. Weights are not in Git.
- **Costs:** `latency_valid.json` (one reply at a time) and `bank_check.json` (hot-swap equals fresh load; swap time; memory).
- **Addenda:**
  - `addenda/indep_rescore.json`: the Phase 4 independent questions with strict and content-lenient 旬空 scoring.
  - `addenda/checker_flags_valid.json`: 30 claim-checker flags read by hand.
  - `addenda/extras.json`: format compliance, loops and titles, and adapter routing simulated from stored replies.
- `phase5/cache/` (not in Git) caches every reply by prompt hash, so reruns reuse it.

## Phase 4 (`phase4/`): training versus deterministic verification

`phase4/RESULTS.md` and `phase4/summary.json` are generated by `scripts/phase4/make_report.py` from the files below. The design is in `docs/phase4_preregistration.md`, and the narrative in `docs/phase4_report.md`.

- `phase4/error_analysis.json`: the Phase 3 errors, read from saved outputs on inspected splits before any Phase 4 training.
- `phase4/training/bazireason-qwen3-4b-lora-p4-a1/`: plan, loss reports and summary of A1. Weights are not in Git.
- **Selection:**
  - `phase4/valid/<label>_<part>.jsonl`: W0 on the validation split, the only input to the frozen rule.
  - `phase4/selection.json`: the rule's output, which selected Phase 2.
  - `phase4/valid/superseded_question_not_sent/`: the first independent-question run, broken by a harness bug that sent no question. It is kept and not used.
- **Final test:**
  - `phase4/final_test/<label>_<part>.jsonl`: the untouched split, model alone. Each record has the raw reply and its score.
  - `_w1` (verify and repair) and `_w2` (tool check) runs carry `first_output`, `check` and `regenerated`.
  - `phase4/final_test_access.jsonl` logs every access. The `base` rows were added after the candidate runs, as a general-purpose baseline.
- `phase4/v3/`: benchmark_v3 (inspected). A1 strict and reasoned, plus W2 on `tool_corrupted` for Phase 2, round 3 and A1. In those three W2 files, `prompt_version` in each record was corrected after generation to match the manifest; the outputs are unchanged, and each manifest says so.
- `phase4/baziqa/`: BaziQA (inspected). A1 without tools and with the verified chart; the contest guard (`tuned_tools_w3.jsonl`) for Phase 2, round 3 and A1. `summary.json` pairs them with the Phase 3 runs in `phase3/baziqa1024/`.

## Phase 3 (`phase3/`): rule-reasoning adapters

`phase3/RESULTS.md` and `phase3/summary.json` are generated by `scripts/phase3/make_report.py` from the files below. All model rows are real greedy inference on the local MLX model. Each output file has a manifest that records the model, the adapter hash and the dataset hash.

- `phase3/training/<run>/`: plan, every loss report and summary for rounds 1–3 (`p3-r1`, `p3-r2`, `p3-r3`). Weights are not in Git.
- **Adapter choice:**
  - `phase3/selection.json` (round 1 vs round 2) and `phase3/selection_final.json` (round 2 vs round 3): rules written before their candidates were scored, on validation splits only.
  - `phase3/final_choice.json`: the calendar constraint of the brief, applied after the test splits were scored. It moved the final choice from round 2 to round 3 and is labelled as post-selection.
- `phase3/heldout/`:
  - `valid_*` and `p2valid_*`: validation splits, used for selection.
  - `dev_*`: the development split, where errors were read.
  - `p3test_*`: the Phase 3 test split, scored for base, Phase 2 and every round.
  - `p2test_*`, `p2fresh_*`: the two Phase 2 test splits.
  - `p2check_*`: a calendar split generated after round 2 and never inspected before scoring.
- `phase3/v3/`: benchmark_v3, strict and reasoned, scored by the unchanged `bazireason evaluate`. The Phase 2 and base rows are in `phase2/v3/`.
- `phase3/baziqa1024/`: BaziQA with a 1,024-token budget for base, Phase 2, Phase 3 round 2 (`p3/`) and round 3 (`p3r3/`). `summary.json` gives the full, BaZi-only, official-key and paper-year tracks with person-cluster bootstrap intervals. `citations_and_loops.json` counts book names and repetition loops in the replies.

Every score is an exact structured comparison with the engine or the rule code; free text is never graded by similarity. Missing, truncated or unparseable replies count as wrong. BaziQA accuracy is agreement with contest keys, not evidence about destiny.

## Phase 2 (`phase2/`): real local model results

`phase2/RESULTS.md` is generated from the files below by `scripts/phase2/make_report.py`. These are real inference results from a local MLX model (`record_kind: llm`, `synthetic: false`); every run has a manifest with the model and adapter hashes.

- `phase2/model_selection/`: measured bake-off of five candidate models.
- `phase2/training/<run>/`: training plan, every loss report, summary and loss curve for both rounds. Weights are not in Git.
- `phase2/heldout/`: the generated held-out splits. `base`, `tuned` (round 1) and `tuned_v2` on the first test split; `fresh_*` on the split generated after round 1; `valid2_*` on the validation split used to choose the adapter.
- `phase2/v3/`: benchmark_v3 predictions, manifests and scores from the unchanged `bazireason evaluate`. `*_strict` uses the checkpoint's prompts; `*_reasoned` adds one sentence allowing visible reasoning (prompt version `v4-reasoned-information-ladder`).
- `phase2/baziqa/`: the four conditions with the round-1 adapter; `phase2/baziqa_v2/`: the round-2 adapter (its `base_*` files are copies of the same base runs); `phase2/baziqa_budget1024/`: one run of the untuned model with a larger token budget. Replies quote contest questions only through question IDs; the question text stays in the fetched benchmark.

Missing, truncated or unparseable replies count as failures in every table. BaziQA accuracy is agreement with published contest keys, not evidence about destiny.

## benchmark_v3 (current)

- `benchmark_v3/deterministic_baselines.jsonl` and its manifest and scores hold zero-call controls on all 261 cases: `copy_context` (the answer-copy ceiling) and three zero-knowledge floors (`heuristic_surface`, `always_abstain`, `always_answer`). They are executed deterministic baselines, **not language-model results**.
- `benchmark_v3/mock_combined_pilot.jsonl` and its manifest and scores are a synthetic mock run of the 44-case combined pilot (352 mock completions plus 88 deterministic records). They verify the software path only.
- `benchmark_v3/independent_boundaries.json` is the JPL DE421/Skyfield side check of the 40 cases within minutes of a Jie (18 events). lunar-python differs from JPL by -3.12 to +1.32 s, with no disagreements.
- `benchmark_v3/calendar_diagnostics.json` is the same-lineage sxtwl comparison (539/539 day/year/month cells, 225/225 hours), and it replays all 261 labels.

The files below describe the historical 96-case (v1) and 152-case (v2) suites and are kept unchanged.


`calendar_diagnostics.json` and `external_anchor_checks.json` contain real executed **calendar** checks, not model experiments. The former has shared-astronomy limitations; the latter checks only two day labels and minute-level Lichun plausibility. Dataset expected labels are still single-engine references.

`mock_smoke.jsonl`, its manifest and metrics are **SYNTHETIC SOFTWARE FIXTURES**. Do not use their scores as evidence for H1-H3. They do not represent inference by Codex, OpenAI, Anthropic, or a local language model. The `synthetic` flag is preserved in evaluation output.

`pytest.txt`/`pytest.xml`, `ruff.txt` and `format.txt` record actual commands. A failure or missing observation cannot be removed from scientific denominators to inflate scores. Live model experiments and training are NOT RUN. API cost is unmeasured, not zero per model call; no paid model calls were executed in this checkpoint session.
