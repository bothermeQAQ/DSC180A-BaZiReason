# Phase 2 data

## `train_v1/`: fine-tuning dataset `p2_train_v1`

Generated by `bazireason.train_data` (seed 2026111001). Rebuild or verify with `python scripts/phase2/build_dataset.py --check`. `manifest.json` records the seed, engine versions, split years, exclusion-list hashes, file hashes and counts.

| Split | Rows | Groups | Years | Use |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| `train.jsonl` | 4,343 | 3,837 | 119 years | LoRA training |
| `valid.jsonl` | 261 | 227 | 30 years (1910-12, 1930-32, ... 2090-92) | validation loss and adapter choice |
| `test.jsonl` | 633 | 548 | 50 years (1915-19, 1935-39, ... 2095-99) | held-out evaluation only |

Each row has `messages` (system, user turns, one reference assistant answer), `family`, `task`, `mode` (which tool output the user turn carries) and `meta` (the structured expected answer and the inputs used to compute it).

### How labels are produced

No label is written by hand and no text is copied from a book, website or contest.

- **Calendar answers** come from the unchanged engine (`bazireason.calendar_engine`, lunar-python 1.4.8, fixed UTC+08:00 profile). The answer text re-derives every pillar by formula (`bazireason.worked`); generation fails if the derivation and the engine disagree.
- **Rule answers** come from `bazireason.rules`, whose tables are checked against lunar-python where the library has the same table. `knowledge/rules_v1.json` is the exported rule collection with the kind and provenance of every rule.
- **Without tools** the expected answer is `needs_tool` exactly when the result depends on the instant of a solar term or on a UTC offset outside a short list of well-known cases (`bazireason.worked.no_tool_expected`). The model is therefore never trained to state a fact it could not know.
- **Requests for personal predictions** (marriage, wealth, health and similar) have one kind of target: what the chart shows, what the tradition associates with it, and a statement that the event cannot be verified. No outcome for any person is invented.

### Leakage guards (tested in `tests/test_phase2.py`)

1. **Year blocks.** The three splits use disjoint calendar years.
2. **Benchmark isolation.** No row is within seven days of any case in `data/synthetic/*.jsonl` (2,719 blocked dates together with guard 3).
3. **Contest isolation.** No row is within one day of a birth date in the BaziQA data. `exclusions/contest_birth_dates.json` holds those 165 dates and nothing else. The generator never reads a contest question, option or answer, and a test confirms no contest question text occurs in the dataset.
4. **One family per date** within a split.
5. **Unseen surface forms.** Two chart wordings, one wording per rule task, four time zones and three invalid zone names occur only in the test split.
6. **Known overlap.** Requests that contain no datetime at all cannot be separated by date. Two such prompts coincide between training and validation; none coincides with the test split, where they use test-only wording.

### Limits

- Templated Chinese. The prompts vary in wording, but the reference answers follow fixed sentence patterns, so the dataset teaches a procedure and a format more than free conversation.
- Labels inherit the engine's profile: a fixed UTC+08:00 reference clock, not local or true solar time.
- The pattern label and the strength count are declared simplifications; the dataset teaches them as conventions with a stated caveat.
- One epoch sees each row once. See `results/phase2/RESULTS.md` for which skills that was and was not enough for.

## `exclusions/`

`contest_birth_dates.json`: birth dates (no names, questions or answers) taken from BaziQA commit `3bad3de`, used only to keep those dates out of the generated data.

## `knowledge/`

`rules_v1.json`: the 18 rule cards and the tables behind them. Kinds: `deterministic_table` (12), `declared_convention` (5), `traditional_association` (1). The statements are this project's paraphrases; the classical works named are in the public domain and cited at the level of the work. No expert has reviewed the chapter-level attribution.
